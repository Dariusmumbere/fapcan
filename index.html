Who We Are

A Registered CBO
Built on Faith & Service

The name FATIMA comes from Portugal where the Virgin Mary appeared to three children in 1917, emphasising the power of prayer. This inspired our founder to create a centre dedicated to prayer and community transformation.

Located in Kidodo Cell, Central Division, Kasese Municipality, Uganda, we serve as a "Home for All" — regardless of appearance, culture, tribe, gender, age, religion or education level. FAPCAN is a registered Community Based Organisation (CBO).

Our Vision

Spiritual empowerment for orphans, PWDs, elders and widows.

Our Mission

Building a strong praying working team to pool resources.

Core Values

Inclusivity, compassion, faith, transparency, and service.

Our Reach

Serving Kasese District and beyond with 300+ members.

Support & Community Transformation

Our Impact in Action

At FAPCAN, compassion is not just a word — it is a daily practice. From supporting persons with albinism and caring for the mentally ill, to sponsoring children's education and uplifting elders, single mothers, and persons with disabilities, we are committed to transforming lives through faith, restoration, and community empowerment.

 Inclusion & Advocacy

Support for People with Albinism

FAPCAN stands as a safe haven for persons with albinism — offering care, dignity, and belonging in a world that too often marginalises them. Through community awareness, advocacy, and daily support, we champion their right to be seen, heard, and fully included.

Care & Protection Inclusion Advocacy
 Healing & Restoration

Transformation Through Prayer & Rehabilitation

We walk alongside those broken by mental illness, addiction, and life's hardest seasons. Through prayer, counselling, and structured rehabilitation, FAPCAN brings restoration, hope, and a renewed sense of purpose.

Mental Health Rehabilitation Hope & Restoration
 Education & Empowerment

Supporting Education for Vulnerable Children

Every child deserves a chance to learn. FAPCAN provides school sponsorships, learning materials, and mentorship to vulnerable children — unlocking doors to knowledge, confidence, and a future full of possibility.

School Sponsorship Empowerment Child Development
 Humanitarian Outreach

Supporting Elders, Single Mothers & Persons with Disabilities

Our community outreach reaches those society often forgets — the elderly, single mothers, and persons with disabilities. FAPCAN provides caregiving, empowerment programs, and a community of belonging.

Our Founder

Alfred Bwambale

Founder & Spiritual Leader — Omuzana

Alfred Bwambale, known as Omuzana, is a devout Catholic endowed with a special gift from God — the gift of prayer and healing that draws people from across Uganda and beyond.

Alfred owns the FAPCAN church where he prays for people of different religions. Beyond being a prayer warrior, he is a father to over 250 children — providing food, shelter, education, skills, and inspiration since 2007.
