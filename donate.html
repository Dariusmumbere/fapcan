Immediate Needs
Our community cries for dignity

16 Causes — Your support changes lives

Shelter, land, medical care, education, farming, worship, recovery — these are not just materials, they are foundations of a new tomorrow.

🏠
Shelter and Land for Dignified Living

We currently sleep in a very compact and limited space, which affects comfort, privacy, and health. We urgently need land to build proper shelters where our community can live safely, with dignity, and in better living conditions. A secure home is the foundation of healing and stability.

Support this need
🎥
Film Equipment for Storytelling

We use film to tell real stories of our people—the pain, the hope, and the transformation within our community. We lack professional cameras, lighting, sound equipment, and editing tools. With proper equipment, we can amplify unheard voices and inspire change across the world.

Support this need
⚕️
Medical Doctors & Legal Support

Our community often needs medical and legal assistance, but it is difficult to consistently afford professionals. We need committed doctors and lawyers — or partners who can support us — so that our people receive proper healthcare, legal protection, and justice when needed.

Support this need
🌾
Land for Agriculture (About 20 Acres)

As a community, we are farmers. We need at least 20 acres of land to grow food for our people. This will help us fight hunger, reduce dependency, and create sustainable food security for children, elders, and vulnerable members.

Support this need
🚜
Transport Means for Farming & Movement

Our farms are far from our home base, making it difficult to dig, plant, and harvest. We urgently need transport means such as a vehicle or motorcycles to ease movement, improve productivity, and support our agricultural work.

Support this need
📽️
Projector for Learning and Awareness

We need a projector to help us educate, train, and keep our community informed. It will be used for learning sessions, awareness programs, film screenings, and keeping people engaged, especially the youth.

Support this need
🐟
Support for Community Projects

We are building sustainable projects such as fish ponds, animal rearing, and poultry farming. These projects need support to grow so that they can provide food, income, and long-term sustainability for our community.

Support this need
📚
School for Elderly Education

We believe learning has no age limit. We want to establish a school for elderly persons where they can learn basic literacy, life skills, and feel empowered, valued, and included in society.

Support this need
🛠️
Community Skills Development Center

We need a skills center where youth and vulnerable individuals can learn practical skills such as tailoring, carpentry, farming techniques, filmmaking, and entrepreneurship to improve their livelihoods.

Support this need
🧑‍⚕️
More HIV Counselors

We need trained HIV counselors to support people living with HIV/AIDS. This will improve emotional care, medication adherence, and ensure that more people receive proper guidance, counseling, and support in accessing ARVs and living positively.

Support this need
👕
Clothing for Vulnerable People

Many people in our community lack proper clothing. We need support in providing clothes to children, the elderly, and vulnerable individuals to restore dignity, comfort, and confidence.

Support this need
🛏️
Mattresses for Better Rest

We need mattresses to improve sleeping conditions. Proper rest is essential for health, recovery, and mental well-being, especially for the sick and elderly.

Support this need
⛪
House of Worship

We desire a dedicated house of worship where people of all faiths can gather in peace, pray, and find spiritual strength together in unity.

Support this need
💡
Support for Utility Bills

Electricity and water bills are challenging for our community to manage. We need support to ensure continuity of essential services that keep our programs running effectively.

Support this need
🌸
Sanitary Pads for Women and Girls

We need sanitary pads to support women and girls with dignity, hygiene, and confidence, especially those from vulnerable backgrounds who cannot afford them.

Support this need
🕊️
Rehabilitation Center

We aim to establish a rehabilitation center to support people struggling with addiction, trauma, and recovery challenges. This center will provide counseling, healing, and reintegration into society.

Support this need
