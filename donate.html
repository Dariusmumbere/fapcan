Your Impact

See What You Can Do

Every shilling given to FAPCAN is used directly to serve those in need. Here is what different levels of giving make possible in Kasese, Uganda.

$10 / month
Provides daily meals for a child for a week and covers basic hygiene supplies.
$30 / month
Covers a child's school materials and uniform for one term of education.
$60 / month
Funds medical check-ups and ARV support for a person living with HIV for one month.
$100 / month
Supports vocational training for a young person, opening doors to independence and livelihood.
