Our Approach

Work as Worship,
Skills as Service

At FAPCAN, every programme is rooted in dignity, sustainability, and inclusion — welcoming PWDs, widows, orphans, and youth to grow together.

Sustainability

All activities generate food and income for long-term resilience.

Inclusivity

Open to all, regardless of background or ability.

Heritage
