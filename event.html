Three Days of Celebration

From the 7th to the 9th of May 2026, FAPCAN hosted a landmark beauty and talent pageant that brought the entire community together in joy, pride, and purpose.

Day One · Wednesday 7th May
Mr. FAPCAN

Wednesday marked the beginning of the event with the competition for boys under the title "Mr. FAPCAN." Young boys confidently showcased their leadership abilities, talents, discipline, and communication skills before the entire community — a powerful display of purpose and character.

Winner: Bwambale Moses

Girls & Women Heats

The Story Behind the Pageant

Born from Inspiration & Courage

This initiative was inspired by the courage and success of one of the FAPCAN girls who previously participated in an outside competition and emerged as the 1st Runner Up. Her achievement gave hope, confidence, and a new image to the FAPCAN community, proving that young girls from the community can shine on bigger platforms.

"Because of that inspiration, FAPCAN officially launched its own annual Beauty and Talent Pageant to continue nurturing girls and boys, shaping them for future leadership, talent development, public speaking, modelling, cultural preservation, and national representation."

The event deeply moved mothers and elderly women in the community, who watched young girls present themselves with dignity, intelligence, creativity, and cultural pride. For the Bakonzo community and the entire Rwenzori region, this became a strong message that culture, talent, and unity can shape a better future.

Cultural Pride

Honouring Bakonzo heritage through traditional wear and performance.

Leadership

Shaping young people into future leaders and community ambassadors.

Empowerment

Building confidence so girls and women know they can stand on bigger stages.

Purpose

Encouraging positive behaviour through talent, education, and transformation.

An Annual Tradition

This event has now become a symbol of hope, unity, empowerment, and transformation within the FAPCAN community and beyond. It is expected to happen every year, bringing more opportunities to young people while promoting the beauty of purpose, culture, talent, and humanity.


Big prizes and opportunities were awarded to the winners and participants as encouragement to continue pursuing their dreams and to inspire those who will follow after them.

Event Host

Alfred Bwambale

FAPCAN Leader & Founder — Omuzana

The Beauty Pageant was successfully hosted by Alfred Bwambale, the FAPCAN leader and founder, known by the community as Omuzana. Under his visionary leadership, this colourful and inspiring event became one of the most memorable celebrations in the FAPCAN community's history.

Alfred's commitment to nurturing young people — their talent, their identity, and their potential — is at the heart of this annual pageant. He believes that every young person, regardless of background, deserves a platform to shine.
